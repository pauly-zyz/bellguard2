How it works

One weekend, start to finish

Everything runs in a Uniswap v4 hook and three small contracts. No off-chain promises: the calendar, the premium and the payout rules are all on-chain.

1 Saturday · the bell

Market closes

Minting and redeeming stop and the Chainlink stock feeds pause. Bellguard pools start charging a premium on each swap.

2 All weekend

Premiums fill the vault

Premiums in USDG go to the cover vault. Underwriters earn them and can't withdraw while cover is live.

3 Monday · reopen + 1h

The gap is settled

A keeper settles each pool against the Chainlink price. That fixes what weekend trading cost liquidity providers.

4 Within 2 days

LPs claim

Positions held through the weekend claim the loss above a 1% deductible, up to 5% of the position.

So far, weekend fees have outweighed gap losses and no gap reached 2%, so none of these weekends would have paid out. Bellguard is cover for the tail: rare, large reopen gaps such as earnings surprises. That's why the premium is low and the deductible sits at 1%. Method and code: research/gapscan.

Two sides of the cover

Provide liquidity, or underwrite it

Liquidity providers

Add liquidity to a Bellguard stock/USDG pool on Uniswap v4. You're covered automatically and pay nothing: weekend traders fund the cover.

  • Keep the position unchanged from before the close until you claim.
  • Your range must cover every price traded during the window.
  • Claims open an hour after reopen and stay open for two days.

Underwriters

Deposit USDG into the cover vault (bgUSDG, ERC-4626). Premiums from closed-market swaps raise the share price; claims lower it.

  • Deposits are open any time.
  • Withdrawals pause while the market is closed and for four days after reopen.
  • One window can pay out at most 20% of the vault; larger claims are paid pro rata.
