How it works

One weekend, start to finish

1

Saturday 02:00 CET: the bell rings

Robinhood stops minting and redeeming stock tokens, and the Chainlink stock feeds pause. Bellguard pools start charging a premium on each swap (0.10% by default, more around earnings) and log what liquidity providers trade.

2

Premiums fill the vault

Premiums paid in USDG go straight to the cover vault, where underwriters earn them. While the market is closed and claims are open, underwriters can't withdraw, so the cover is always there when it's needed.

3

Monday: the gap is settled

An hour after reopen, the Chainlink price fixes what weekend trading cost each pool. Positions left untouched all weekend claim their loss above a 1% deductible, up to 5% of the position.

Two sides of the cover

Provide liquidity, or underwrite it

Liquidity providers

Add liquidity to a Bellguard stock/USDG pool on Uniswap v4. You're covered automatically. You pay nothing; weekend traders fund the cover.

  • Keep the position unchanged from before the close until you claim.
  • Your range must cover every price traded during the window.
  • Claims open an hour after reopen and stay open for two days.
Deductible
1% of position value

Reference price
Chainlink, after reopen

Underwriters

Deposit USDG into the cover vault (bgUSDG, ERC-4626). Premiums from closed-market swaps raise the share price; claims lower it.

  • Deposits are open any time.
  • Withdrawals pause while the market is closed and for four days after reopen.
  • One window can pay out at most 20% of the vault. Larger claims are paid pro rata.
