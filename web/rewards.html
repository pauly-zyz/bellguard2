Fees are counted live from Bellguard Perps. Rewards for a week are calculated after it ends (Monday 00:00 UTC) and can be claimed once published.

How it works

  1. Hold ≥ 1,000,000 $BLGD. Eligibility uses your average balance over the week (time-weighted), so buying just before the week ends doesn't count. Contracts and burn addresses are excluded.
  2. The pool is the week's trading fees. Open, close and borrow fees from every long and short on Bellguard Perps. Launch week: 100% of fees.
  3. Traders first. Eligible holders who traded get their own fees back in full.
  4. Then all holders. Fees paid by everyone else are shared among eligible holders by average balance.
  5. Claim in USDC on this page, any time after the week is published. One claim collects every unclaimed week.

Rewards are a fee rebate funded from trading fees, not a return on holding a token. Amounts are calculated from on-chain data; the weekly files are public in the project repository.
